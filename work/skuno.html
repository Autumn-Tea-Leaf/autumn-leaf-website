Skuno

Skuno imagines UNO and Skittles as one deck: a card game that tastes the rainbow.

The wordmark folds the two names together, and a single strip of the Skittles rainbow drips from the N. Everything else is pared back to charcoal cards with coral corners and bold numerals, so the colour belongs to the brand moment rather than the noise.
