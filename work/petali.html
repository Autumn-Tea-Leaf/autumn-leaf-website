Petali

Petali: the calm that carries weight. A conceptual botanical soda brand exploring soft strength: a grounded confidence that doesn’t need to be loud to be felt.

The project spans branding, packaging and visual identity, chasing elegance, freshness and modern minimalism. Every flavour and detail was tuned for balance and quiet clarity, proof that lightness can still hold power.
