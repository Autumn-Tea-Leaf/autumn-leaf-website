True Threads

True Threads sheds light on the hidden cost of fast fashion.

A clean, intuitive site leads visitors through the realities of sweatshop labour, told through “Steve,” a fictional but relatable worker. A world map pinpoints the global impact, and the journey closes by pointing people towards sustainable brands. Storytelling that moves from awareness to action.
