Moon + Son

A landing page for Moon + Son, a wool and crochet store in Craighall Park. The line: patch it up with us.

Soft greys and a warm yarn yellow carry the store’s logo through the whole page. A single strand of yarn winds from section to section, past balls of wool, a patchwork cat in a knitted scarf, a quilted doll and other hand-drawn crochet characters, ending at the shop’s location and a Join Us call to action.
