Zukae

Zukae is a brutalist-inspired instant noodle concept built on contrast, silence and hunger. Restraint, then release.

Stark design, flash photography and heavy visual minimalism carry raw emotion. “Silence is heavy. Hunger breaks you.” Each visual explores the tension between control and chaos, turning a cup of noodles into a symbol of craving, pressure and power.
