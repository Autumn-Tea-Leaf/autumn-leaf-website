AR Zen Garden

An augmented-reality Zen garden that brings tranquility into any space.

A central tree anchors raked sand and carefully placed stones, symbols of harmony and order, while soft ambient music completes the calm. Built for Echo3D, it invites anyone to step into a customisable virtual retreat, Zen principles meeting modern technology.
