Comic Con Johannesburg

A retro arcade campaign for Comic Con Johannesburg, which placed third in the Comic Con Africa design competition.

Five pixel characters, a cat, a ghost, a TV, a game console and an alien, lead the whole campaign. Each one fronts a different part of the show (comics, cosplay, anime and K-pop, gaming, TV and movies) and appears across an Instagram series, a ticket, QR codes, stickers, an arcade cabinet and a digital leaderboard. The message to every visitor: begin quest.
