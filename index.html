(04) About

Rooted in porcelain 瓷

Andy Ou, founder of Autumn Leaf

I’m Andy Ou, a digital designer of Chinese heritage based in Johannesburg. Autumn Leaf is my studio, a place where tradition shapes modern living.

Years ago my father walked me through a museum of porcelain. The blue-and-white glaze, the calm confidence of a vase that needs no ornament. It never left me.

Today that feeling guides every brief: muted tones, organic forms and refined craft, applied to identities, packaging, interfaces and motion that stay calm while the world gets louder.
