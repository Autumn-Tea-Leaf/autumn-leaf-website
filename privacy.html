(秋叶) Privacy

Privacy notice

The short version: this site doesn’t track you. If you send the contact form or email me, I only use your details to reply.

Last updated 11 October 2026
01

Who I am

This site belongs to Andy Ou, trading as Autumn Leaf Design Studio, based in Johannesburg, South Africa. For anything about your personal information, email autumnleafdesignstudio@gmail.com.

02

What this site collects

Nothing in the background. There are no cookies, no analytics and no advertising trackers. Fonts and scripts are served from this site rather than from outside services. Like every website, the hosting provider (GitHub Pages) briefly logs technical details such as IP addresses to keep the service secure.

03

Browser storage

The site saves one small note in your browser so the intro animation only plays once per visit. It is not a cookie, it is never sent anywhere, and it clears itself when you close the tab. If you send the contact form, your browser also keeps the times of your last few messages for a day, to stop spam floods. That note holds no names or messages and is never sent anywhere. If you switch between light and dark mode, your choice is remembered the same way.

04

The contact form

When you send the contact form, your name, email address, phone number (if you add one), the type of project and your message are emailed to me through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a service that forwards form messages to my inbox. Nothing is stored on this site itself.

05

When you email me

I receive your name, email address and whatever you choose to write. I use it only to reply and to work with you on your project. I never sell it or share it for marketing. I keep our emails for as long as we are working together and afterwards only as long as I need them for my records, and I will delete them sooner if you ask.

06

Third-party content

The Vega project page includes a video player from Adobe (Behance), which may set its own cookies when it loads. Links to Behance, LinkedIn and Instagram take you to sites with their own privacy policies.

07

Your rights

Under South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) you can ask to see the information I hold about you, have it corrected, or have it deleted. Just email me. If you are unhappy with how your information was handled, you can contact the Information Regulator.

08

Copyright

All projects, photographs, illustrations, animations and designs on this site belong to Andy Ou and, where credited, his collaborators. Please ask before using any of them.
